## MacDock v1.7
### Added
- **Folder fan redesign**: labels lean along the arc, thumbnails tilt like the macOS Dock, and an "Open in Explorer" arrow sits at the top of the fan.
- **Click-to-pin folder fans**: click a folder stack to open its fan and keep it open; click the folder or the chevron tile to fold it back.
- **Dock tile chevron** with smooth fade/scale while a fan is open.
- **Emoji in Dynamic Island** notifications.
- **Uninstall from Launchpad** finds uninstallers next to apps that don't register one.

### Changed
- LocalSend: TLS 1.2 + 1.3, no Expect: 100-continue (better compatibility with newer peers).
- Icons are never blank: transparent icons fall back to a tile with the app's initial.

Install: extract `MacDock-v1.7.zip` and run `Install.bat`.
