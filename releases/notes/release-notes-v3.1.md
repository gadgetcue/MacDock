## MacDock v3.1
### New
- **Window previews, upgraded**: each preview has a title row with a close button, fades in and out, and peeks at the window you hover. Clicking an app with several windows shows its previews.
- **Show previews: On hover / On click**, a new choice in Settings.
- **Switch sound output** by clicking a device in the Sound panel.

### Improved
- **Settings regrouped** into clearer categories (including Windows & Previews, Dynamic Island, Control Center and Shortcuts & Gestures), with macOS 27 style pop-up menus.
- **Send with LocalSend** (right-click): folders can be sent, large selections keep the menu entry, items picked together or in quick succession collect in one popup, and no second MacDock process is started per send.
- **Sound panel** follows volume and mute changes made elsewhere while it is open.
- Donation card simplified.

### Fixed
- **Faster app launches**: apps opened from the dock no longer inherit the low disk and memory priority of the startup task.
- **Settings search** finds individual settings and jumps to them.
- **Sound panel**: the output device list no longer fails or stays on "Loading...", and a refresh no longer drops the slider you are dragging.
- **Night Light**: the switch and the strength slider now change the screen right away.
- **Peek** no longer hides the dock, menu bar or island.
- The old "MacDock LocalSend" *Send to* shortcut, which opened Windows' "Open with" box, is repaired at startup.
